Support

Mahjong Garden · iOS

Mahjong Garden is a single-player mahjong-inspired block puzzle game for iPhone and iPad.

Gameplay Help

  • Drag tile sets onto the board to place them.
  • Fill a full row or column to clear tiles and score points.
  • Rewarded ads are optional and may provide in-game help such as rotate, swap, continue, or daily bonus rewards.
  • Coins, best score, unlocked skins, and daily reward status are stored locally on your device.

Common Issues

  • If an ad is not available, wait a moment and try again. Ad availability can vary by region, network, and ad provider status.
  • If progress appears missing, confirm the app was not deleted or reinstalled. Local progress can be removed when the app is deleted.
  • If the game layout looks unusual, close and reopen the app, then make sure your device is running a current iOS version.

Privacy

For details about local game progress, advertising, and privacy choices, see the Privacy Policy.
