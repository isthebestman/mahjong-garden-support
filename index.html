Privacy Policy

Mahjong Garden · Effective date: May 22, 2026

Mahjong Garden is a casual mahjong tile puzzle game. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, and what choices players have.

Information We Do Not Collect Directly

We do not require account registration and we do not ask players to provide a name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.

Game Progress Stored On Your Device

The game stores basic gameplay information on your device, such as score, best score, coins, unlocked skins, rotate rewards, and daily reward status. This information is used only to run gameplay features and is not sent to our own servers.

Advertising

Mahjong Garden uses Google AdMob to show ads, including rewarded ads. Google and its partners may collect or receive information from your device, including device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, app interactions, ad views, and ad performance data.

Google may use this information to provide, limit, measure, and improve advertising. You can learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services at Google's partner sites policy.

Analytics

We do not currently use a separate analytics service. Advertising providers may still collect ad-related diagnostics and performance data as described above.

Children's Privacy

Mahjong Garden is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us through the support information listed on the App Store product page.

Your Choices

  • You can reset or limit advertising tracking through your device privacy settings.
  • You can delete locally stored game progress by deleting the app from your device.
  • You can choose whether to watch rewarded ads. Rewarded ads are optional.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Advertising-related data may be processed by Google AdMob and its partners according to their own policies and applicable law.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game changes, when advertising services change, or when legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when changes are made.

Contact

If you have privacy questions, please contact us at lishop92@outlook.com.

This policy applies to Mahjong Garden for iOS.
